The useful details.

What stays on my device?

Projects and screenshots live in your browser. Export a project backup before clearing site data or changing browsers. Optional AI sends your description and captions to your chosen provider.

Do I need an AI key?

No. The editor and exports work without AI. Use your own Anthropic key for optional captions, or copy the prompt into your preferred assistant.

Can I use this commercially?

Yes. The source is MIT licensed. Your exported designs can be used for your apps. You remain responsible for rights to uploaded material and truthful marketing claims.

Will these assets be accepted by Apple?

Framegrove exports the preset dimensions and opaque Creative Assets PNGs. Review your content, cropping and current store requirements in App Store Connect before submission.

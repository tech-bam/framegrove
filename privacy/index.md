# Privacy and usage

Projects and screenshots are stored locally in browser IndexedDB. Framegrove does not upload them to its own server. Export a project backup before clearing site data or changing browsers.

Optional AI requests send descriptions and captions directly to Anthropic with your own key. AI provider fees and terms apply. The editor and export work without AI.

Cloudflare hosts the website and processes ordinary connection data. Google Fonts supplies web fonts. Support payments occur on Buy Me a Coffee under its own terms; Framegrove does not receive card data.

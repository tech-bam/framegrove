Your projects stay with you.

Framegrove stores projects and imported screenshots in your browser’s IndexedDB. The editor does not upload them to a Framegrove server. Export project backups before clearing site data or changing browsers.

Optional AI

If you choose to use the AI panel, your description and captions are sent directly to the selected provider using your own API key. Those requests are subject to the provider’s privacy policy and charges. You can use the editor without AI.

Website services

The website is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes standard connection information to deliver the site. Google Fonts supplies web fonts. Coffee payments happen on Buy Me a Coffee under its own terms; Framegrove does not receive your payment card details.

Usage

The editor is free to use. You are responsible for rights to uploaded material, accuracy of your marketing claims and compliance with store requirements. Preview exported assets in the target store before submission. Optional coffee contributions do not unlock a subscription or paid feature.

# App Store Creative Assets

Creative Assets are static promotional artwork for the App Store product page header and search results. They are separate from screenshot sets and preview videos.

| Placement | Export size | Format | Output ID |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Header 21:9 | 3840 × 1646 | PNG | apple-header |
| Search 3:2 | 3840 × 2560 | PNG or JPG | apple-search |
| Header + Search 16:9 | 5244 × 2950 | PNG | apple-universal |

Use a dedicated placement composition, accurate copy and your real app screen. Framegrove emits opaque RGB PNGs without an alpha channel for Header and Universal. Search supports PNG or JPG. Review cropping across devices in App Store Connect.

[Apple Creative Assets specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/creative-assets-specifications). [Choose creative templates](https://framegrove.bamstudio.dev/templates/#/creative).

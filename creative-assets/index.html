Make it a brand moment.

  1. Choose a Header, Search or Universal template.
  2. Add a real screenshot, a short benefit and your brand colours. All layers stay editable.
  3. Export the exact placement size. Preview it in App Store Connect and check device-specific cropping before submission.

Avoid tiny text near the edges. Keep the focal point clear, and only make claims your app supports. Matching your screenshot series and Creative Assets helps make your store presence feel coherent.

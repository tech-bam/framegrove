# Framegrove

Free, MIT-licensed, local-first editor for App Store Creative Assets, iPhone Duo screenshots and App Store/Google Play screenshot campaigns. No signup, subscription or watermark. Optional support: https://buymeacoffee.com/bamstudio

## Capabilities

56 curated templates: 16 phone collections (6 editable screens each), 4 tablet collections, 24 Apple Creative Assets compositions and 12 Duo collections (3 screens each). Each template declares its own output sizes and orientation. Projects and screenshots are stored in browser IndexedDB and can be backed up as `.sms.json` files.

The website supports English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Japanese. Turkish browser visitors start in Turkish; other browsers start in English unless a supported language was explicitly selected. Captions can be maintained in 34 project languages. Sample template copy is EN/TR; localization remains editable.

## Availability and scope

Framegrove renders marketing images; it does not validate app compatibility, upload to stores, or guarantee review approval or search ranking. Apple is not affiliated with Framegrove. Store rules and upload availability must be checked in App Store Connect.
